ATS RESUME GUIDANCE
- Use standard section headings such as Summary, Experience, Education, Skills, Projects, and Certifications.
- Use simple readable formatting and avoid unnecessary graphics, text boxes, icons, and decorative elements.
- Use job-relevant keywords naturally and truthfully.
- Prefer measurable achievements where possible.
- Keep dates, job titles, employers, and locations consistent.
- Do not add skills, qualifications, or experience that the candidate does not actually have.
